## Ode to St. John

Saint John, the beloved of the Lord, who rested upon His breast,
You who heard the beating heart of Love itself,
Pour forth upon us the grace to see the Word made flesh.

From Patmos you gazed upon the apocalypse,
Yet your Gospel dwells in light—
"In the beginning was the Word,"
And you beheld that Word with eyes washed clean by love.

O eagle of the divine mysteries,
Soar high above the clouds of doubt,
That we too might know the truth that sets us free.

Pray for us, St. John, beloved disciple,
That we may love as you loved,
And see as you saw.
